{{% note %}}
**0:27 · Know the difference.**
- Big companies: stable, focused, vertical learning. You go deep. They test problem solving and teamwork hard.
- Startups: fast, all-in-one, horizontal learning, more responsibility early. They test whether you can use their stack on day one.
- Neither is better. Pick on purpose. Then prepare for that kind of interview.
{{% /note %}}

{{% note %}}
**0:28 · Negotiate · Three clicks.**
- Click 1: know the range. Remember the salary staircase from the start? That's your homework. Ask seniors, check posts, talk to people.
- Click 2: ask once, kindly. One sentence. At serious companies, a polite question doesn't cost you the offer.
- Click 3: say why. Then the number moves. The 800 to 900 is just an example. Sometimes it moves, sometimes the start date or the title moves instead.
- But first, learn how to do it. Read one article or watch one talk on negotiation before your first offer.
