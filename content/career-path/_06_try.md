{{% note %}}
**0:35 · One example path, not the only one.**
- Year one and two: try a different path every semester. Small projects. A weekend, a course, a hackathon.
- Year three: notice which one keeps coming back. That's your signal. The amber ones.
- Year four: go deep. Internship and graduation project on the same path.
- First years: you are right on time. Fourth years: you can still do two tries this semester. Not too late.
