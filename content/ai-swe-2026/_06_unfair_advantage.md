{{% note %}}
**0:35 – 0:37 · Tie back to the junior bar**
- 01 context: the AI only knows what you give it. That's the "tell it what matters" skill.
- 02 communication: the new bar from Act 2. Organize the thought out loud. Interviews and pull requests both ask for this.
- 03 verification: proving correctness, not hoping. This is why DSA, SQL, and reading code still matter — you need something to check against.
- 04 system thinking: debug the loop, and decide what is worth building. A fast feature nobody needs is still a bad feature.
{{% /note %}}
