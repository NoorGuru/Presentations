  <div class="verbs">
    <div class="fragment"><h2 class="t-big">Brief it.</h2><p class="verb-say">Give it the rules and what “done” means.</p></div>
    <div class="fragment"><h2 class="t-big">Say it.</h2><p class="verb-say">Explain the change so a teammate can follow.</p></div>
    <div class="fragment"><h2 class="t-big">Prove it.</h2><p class="verb-say">Catch the bad change before it ships.</p></div>
    <div class="fragment"><h2 class="t-big">See it.</h2><p class="verb-say">Understand the system. Decide what to build.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

{{% note %}}
**0:29 – 0:31 · Four clicks. This is the new bar above the foundation, said as verbs.**
- **Brief it** = use AI on purpose. The AI only knows what you give it.
- **Say it** = explain it out loud. Interviews and pull requests both ask for this.
- **Prove it** = prove it's right. Why DSA, SQL, and reading code still matter: you need something to check against.
- **See it:** the one AI can't do for you. A fast feature nobody needs is still a bad feature.
{{% /note %}}
