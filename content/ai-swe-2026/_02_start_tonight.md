{{% note %}}
**0:05 – 0:10 · Three Blunt Habits**
- **01 Build Proof:** Stop submitting `FinalProject_v3_FINAL.zip` and forgetting it. Public repos, clean commit history, live demo link, readable README.
- **02 Flip AI Usage:** Copy-pasting homework trains you to fail technical interviews and break production. Flip the prompt: *"Here is my design — roast it, break it, find concurrency and auth edge cases."*
- **03 Reading Muscle:** Open real open-source repos, read PR diffs, practice reviewing code you didn't write. That's 80%+ of your job now.
