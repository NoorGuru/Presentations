Every shortcut

Moving through the talk

  • SpaceNPgDnNext step. Lines that appear come first, then the next slide. Click does this too.
  • Shift SpacePPgUpPrevious step.
  • →LNext section.
  • ←HPrevious section.
  • ↓JDown inside this section.
  • ↑KUp inside this section.
  • Alt+arrowsChange slides and skip lines that appear.
  • HomeFirst section.
  • EndLast section.
  • Shift+←→First or last section.
  • Shift+↑↓First or last slide in this section.

In the room

  • FFullscreen.
  • SSpeaker notes, in another window.
  • B.Blank the screen. Press again to come back.
  • EscOSee every slide. Esc again to return.
  • GJump to a slide number.
  • ?Open or close this list.
